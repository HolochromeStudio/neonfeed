## Statuses

- **Slow** - Moves slower.
- **Stun** - Cannot move or attack.
- **Burn** - Takes fire damage over time.
- **Wet** - Soaked. Shock chains harder. Extinguishes Burn.
- **Shock** - Takes extra damage from electric hits.
- **Marked** - Takes +30% damage (police +20% more).
- **Armor Break** - Armor halved.
- **Pushback** - Shoved backward along the road.
- **Silence** - Abilities disabled.
- **Haste** - Faster movement / attacks.
- **Shield** - Absorbs the next debuff or hit.
- **Jammed** - Attack speed halved.
- **Oil** - Slick: +25% speed, burns hotter.
- **Fear** - Turns around and flees.
