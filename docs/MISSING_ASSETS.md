## PLACEHOLDER sprites (generated, atlas `placeholder`)

Files: `assets/placeholder/<key>.png`, atlas `assets/generated/placeholder_atlas.{png,json}` (256x256, Phaser JSON-hash, 2 px padding). All frames are PLACEHOLDER; most carry a 2x2 magenta pixel in the top-left corner (top-right on `enemy_*`, none on `bullet`) as a visible "placeholder" tell. Never hand-edit; replace by adding real art under the same frame key (or remap in animation defs) and delete the entry here.

